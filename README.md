#E-Commerce Sales Dashboard


 1- Project Title / Headline:
 

🛒 E-Commerce Sales Dashboard: Complete Sales Performance Analysis:

This is a dynamic and interactive Power BI dashboard created to explore e-commerce sales data. It focuses on product performance, category insights, regional sales, and monthly revenue trends from 2022 to 2024.



 2- Short Description / Purpose:
 

The E-Commerce Sales Dashboard is a clean and powerful Power BI report that helps users understand sales performance across 10 products, 3 categories, and 4 regions over 3 years.
It highlights important metrics like total sales, total profit, total quantity, total orders, average order value, and unique products.

This dashboard is helpful for business analysts, sales managers, e-commerce owners, and data learners who want to quickly see what is selling, where it is selling, and how revenue changes over time.



3- Tech Stack:

* 📊 Power BI Desktop – Used to build the visuals and final dashboard

* 🗂️ Power Query – Used for cleaning and preparing the raw dataset

* 🧮 DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) – Used to create KPI cards and measures like Average Order Value and Total Profit

* 📅 Data Modeling – Used to set relationships and filters for smooth cross-filtering

* 📁 File Format – .pbit (Power BI report template)
  


 4- Data Source:

Source: Kaggle —   https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/itszubi/e-commerce-business-profitability-analysis


The dataset contains sales records of 10 products, 3 categories, and 4 regions from 2022 to 2024.
It shows product-wise revenue, profits, and quantity sold.

* Key Fields:

*  Order Date – Date of the order
* Product Name  – Name of the product
* Category – Category (Electronics / Accessories / Office)
* Region – Sales region (North / South / East / West)
* Quantity – Number of units sold
*  Sales – Total revenue
* Profit – Net profit
* Year – Year of the sale



 5- Features / Highlights:

 a-  Business Problem:
 

E-Commerce businesses get thousands of orders every day. Without a central dashboard, it is very hard to know which products sell the best, which regions perform the strongest, and how sales change month to month. Without this understanding, businesses can miss opportunities and make poor inventory or marketing decisions.


b-  Goal of the Dashboard:
  

The goal of this E-Commerce Sales Dashboard is to provide one single, interactive place where anyone can:

* See total sales, profit, quantity, and orders through KPI Cards
* Check yearly sales performance using a Column Chart
* Identify top-selling categories using a horizontal Bar Chart
* Understand regional sales using a Donut Chart
* View month-wise revenue using a Line Chart
* Explore top-performing products using a Table Visual
* Filter data easily using Region, Category, and Year slicers

c-  Walk Through of Key Visuals:

* KPI Cards (Top Row): Show Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Quantity, Total Orders, Avg Order Value, and Unique Products — giving a quick overview of business performance.

* Yearly Sales Performance (Column Chart): Shows how revenue grew from 2022 to 2024 and highlights the best year.

* Sales by Category (Bar Chart):  Compares total sales of Electronics, Accessories, and Office categories — Electronics leads with 49.9% of total revenue.

* Regional Sales Overview (Donut Chart): Shows sales distribution across 4 regions — West leads with 26.7%, followed by East, South, and North.

* Top 6 Products by Sales (Table): Shows product-wise sales and profit. Camera, Monitor, and Mouse are top performers.

* Monthly Sales Trend (Line Chart): Displays month-wise sales, showing seasonal patterns and peak sales months.

* 3 Interactive Slicers (Region, Category, Year): Allow users to filter the dashboard easily and explore any specific region, category, or year.

d-  Business Impact & Insights:

* 🏆 Camera is the highest-selling product, generating **1177K** in sales
* 📦 Electronics is the best category, contributing **49.9%** of total revenue
* 🌍 West region performs the strongest in sales and profit
* 📈 2023 is the best year with **3.8M** revenue
* 💰 Total profit margin is **17.3%**, steady across all categories
* 📅 Monthly trend shows clear peak months, useful for planning stock and marketing
